Open the account menu (your initial, top right) and choose **Account & preferences**.

## Profile

Change your **Name** or **Email address** and select **Save profile**.

If you change your email address, Metribay sends a confirmation link to the new address. Confirm it before you continue using the app.

## Password

Enter your **Current password**, then a **New password** twice, and select **Update password**. Use at least 14 characters. A password manager makes this easy.

### Forgot your password?

Select **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and enter your email address. If it belongs to an account, you'll get a reset link by email. For privacy, the page says the same thing whether or not the address has an account.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication asks for a code from an authenticator app each time you sign in, so a stolen password alone isn't enough. We recommend it for everyone, and especially for workspace owners, who manage provider credentials.

**Turn it on:**

1. Select **Enable two-factor authentication**. You may need to confirm your password first.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, such as 1Password, Google Authenticator or the one built into your phone. Or open **Enter setup key manually**.
3. Enter the 6-digit **Authentication code** from the app and select **Confirm setup**.
4. Open **View recovery codes** and store them somewhere safe, such as your password manager.

**Recovery codes** let you sign in if you lose your phone. Each code works once. Select **Generate new recovery codes** to replace them all.

**Turn it off** with **Disable two-factor authentication**.

:::caution[Lost your phone and your recovery codes?]
Email [rob@worbee.com](mailto:rob@worbee.com) from the address on your account. For your security, we'll need to verify that the account is yours before we can help.
:::

## Appearance

These preferences are saved in your browser on this device, and apply straight away.

- **Color theme:** **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device. The sun icon in the header switches it quickly.
- **Numbers:** **Full numbers** (12,450) or **Compact** (12.5K). Daily value tables always show exact numbers.
- **Overview spacing:** **Comfortable** or **Compact**, to fit more projects on the screen.

Because they're saved per device, you can use compact numbers on your phone and full numbers on your laptop.

## Sign out

Open the account menu and choose **Sign out**.

## Delete your account

Email [rob@worbee.com](mailto:rob@worbee.com) from the address on your account and ask for it to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days. Before that, you may want to [export your configuration](../import-export/). See [Security and privacy](../security/#deleting-your-data).
